About

TAIWAN → BEIJING → SINGAPORE

I'm a game designer and interaction designer with a background in digital media arts, currently based between Beijing and Singapore.

I graduated from Communication University of China with a degree in Digital Media Arts, and I'm currently continuing my studies in the GDAT programme at NTU. I love games — especially strategy RPGs, card games, and puzzle/mystery titles — and that love shows up directly in my work: most of my projects sit at the intersection of game systems and AI-driven interaction.

I've shipped several games and interactive installations that integrate AI features end-to-end, and lately I've been experimenting with using AI directly inside the game-making process itself. On the tool side, I work comfortably across Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, and ComfyUI for AI-assisted creation, and Unity, Unreal Engine 5, Processing, and TouchDesigner for building playable and interactive systems. On the visual side, my strongest craft is interface design — particularly game UI.

Overview

A 2D anime-style side-scrolling roguelike deck-builder set in the world of Cyberpunk 2077, envisioned as a mobile extension of the IP after its main narrative concludes. Players lead a squad through the "Restricted Zone," uncovering the hidden truths that made it forbidden.

I owned the full UI/UX design cycle solo — from competitive analysis of Onmyoji, Reverse:1999, and Arknights roguelike loops, through low-fidelity flow diagrams, to a complete high-fidelity interaction system covering every button state (default / hover / pressed / focus / inactive) across main menu, character equip, map exploration, and turn-based combat. WORK — 03 · AIGC · EEG · INSTALLATION ART

TRANSFER,
REBIRTH自我的重构

An EEG-driven generative AI installation that reads emotion and redraws the face.

SCROLL
CASE STUDY 03
ROLEGroup Project
TIMELINE2024.09 – 2025.06
FOCUSAIGC · EEG · Installation Art
ComfyUITouchDesignerEEG / NeuroSkyMediapipeGenerative AI Pipeline

Overview

An AIGC-driven EEG interaction installation exploring emotional somatization — how suppressed feelings surface as physical symptoms. The system reads a user's brainwave signals, calculates real-time arousal and valence, and uses generative AI to redraw the user's face into an exaggerated emotional mask that the user then has to physically perform to "unlock."

My contribution spanned the full generative pipeline: EEG signal capture via a NeuroSky sensor, an arousal/valence scoring model in Python, a live ComfyUI portrait-generation workflow embedded in TouchDesigner, and Mediapipe-based facial-landmark matching to evaluate whether the user's expression matched the AI-generated target. We iterated through three prototypes — moving from a fragile Sichuan-opera-inspired face-changing gesture to a simple hand-grasp toggle — and exhibited the final installation with a live photo-print station.

A HAND

2024.09 – 2025.06

Give Me a Hand

A desktop smart accessory that supports users through moments of acute anxiety. Built around a GSR sensor, heart-rate sensor, and force-sensing resistor wired to an Arduino UNO, the device reads skin conductance, heart rate, and blood oxygen to detect rising stress. When stress crosses a threshold, an LED-lit hand invites interaction; once held, it shifts color and plays calming music, interrupting the anxiety cycle through touch rather than another screen.
